## Overview

A US retailer's returns were high enough to materially eat into profit. This project is an 8-point Tableau story that investigates the returns problem end to end — measuring its size, locating where it concentrates, and identifying which products, regions, and customer behaviors drive it — then converts the findings into concrete operational recommendations.

## Key findings

- **Overall return rate: 25.97%**, with returned orders costing the business **$142,137**
- The **Machines** sub-category returns at **35.03%** — more than double the next-highest sub-category
- Returns concentrate in the **West region** (Utah, California, and Oregon are the worst-affected states); West Furniture and Office Supplies return rates reach **39.74%** and **42.91%**, versus just 7.52% and 13.51% in the Central region
- Returns show a **seasonal peak in August**
- A small group of **repeat customers return at nearly 100%** of their orders — a pattern flagged for review

## Recommendations

1. Audit Machines product quality, descriptions, and fulfillment — the single highest-leverage fix
2. Investigate West-region logistics and handling, where return rates are dramatically out of line with other regions
3. Flag repeat returners for account review
4. Roll out monthly returns monitoring with an automatic flag when a sub-category crosses a set return-rate threshold

## What the story covers

The Tableau story walks through the analysis in eight steps: overall return rate and cost, sub-category breakdown, regional concentration, state-level detail, category-by-region interaction, seasonal pattern, repeat-customer behavior, and the recommended actions.
